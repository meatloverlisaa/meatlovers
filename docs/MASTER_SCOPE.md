# Meat Lovers CIMS powered by YohPal — Master Scope
## 1. Customer Acquisition Website
The website shall act as an AI-enabled disruptive customer acquisition engine.
It shall support:
- food menu discovery
- bar and drinks visibility
- promotions
- online enquiries
- customer lead capture
- AI assistant / chatbot placeholder
- WhatsApp and call-to-order links
- delivery lead capture
- customer feedback
- event and catering enquiry capture
- customer segmentation source
## 2. Product Segmentation
The system shall separate:
1. Food
2. Soft drinks
3. Alcoholic drinks
Each product must belong to one of these categories.


## 3. Departments Covered
The system shall cover:
1. Suppliers
2. Storekeeping
3. Food production
4. Service
5. Dispatch
6. Bar
7. Unsold cooked food
8. HRM
9. CRM
10. Deliveries
11. Product segmentation
12. Staff motivation, especially service team
13. Asset inventory
14. Finance P&L
15. Daily, weekly, monthly, and annual income statements
## 4. Anti-Theft Controls
The system shall control theft by:
- recording every stock movement
- separating store, kitchen, bar, service, and dispatch stock
- requiring approval for cancellations
- requiring approval for discounts
- recording unsold cooked food
- tracking bar stock separately
- recording cash settlements
- tracking staff actions
- producing daily reports
## 5. Sales Growth Controls
The system shall increase sales through:
- AI-enabled website lead capture
- customer CRM
- promotions
- product performance tracking
- waiter performance tracking
- service motivation dashboard
- delivery tracking
- repeat-customer monitoring
## 6. Mobile-First Rule
The system must work properly on:
- smartphones
- tablets
- laptops
- desktops
Waiters must be able to use smartphones as POS devices.
