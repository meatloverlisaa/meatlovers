# Batch 21 — Production + Menu Engineering Pack
## System
Meat Lovers CIMS powered by YohPal
## Purpose
This batch controls kitchen production, recipes, food cost, and menu profitability.
## Features Added
1. Recipe/BOM setup
2. Recipe ingredients
3. Kitchen production plans
4. Ingredient consumption
5. Food wastage control
6. Food cost per plate
7. Gross margin per menu item
8. Menu engineering report
## Recipe/BOM
Each food menu item can have a recipe defining:


- ingredient product
- quantity required
- unit cost
## Kitchen Production Plans
Production plans track:
- planned quantity
- produced quantity
- sold quantity
- wasted quantity
- production status
## Ingredient Consumption
Ingredient consumption reduces stock quantity.
Sources:
- production plan
- order
- manual adjustment
## Wastage Control
Wastage records:
- product
- quantity wasted
- estimated cost
- reason
- person declaring wastage
## Menu Engineering
The system calculates:
- quantity sold
- sales value
- estimated cost
- gross profit
## Business Value
This helps Meat Lovers:
- know food cost per plate
- identify profitable food items
- reduce kitchen wastage
- control ingredient usage
- stop kitchen stock leakage
- improve menu pricing decisions
