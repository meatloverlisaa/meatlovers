## System

## Purpose
This batch strengthens product segmentation, pricing control, margin protection, and product activation
governance.


## Product Categories
The system enforces three commercial categories:
1. Food
2. Soft drinks
3. Alcoholic drinks
## Pricing Rules
Pricing rules define:
- minimum margin percentage
- maximum discount percentage
- rule status
- notes
## Category Dashboards
Management can view dashboards for:
- food
- soft drinks
- alcoholic drinks
Each dashboard shows:
- product count
- active count
- inactive count
- average margin
- product-level margin
## Price Change Audit
Every price update records:
- old selling price
- new selling price
- old cost price
- new cost price
- user who changed it
- reason for change
