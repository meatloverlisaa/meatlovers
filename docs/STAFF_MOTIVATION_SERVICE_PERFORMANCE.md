# Batch 18 — Staff Motivation + Service Performance Pack
## System
Meat Lovers CIMS powered by YohPal
## Purpose
This batch introduces staff motivation and service performance controls.
## Modules Added
1. Waiter sales leaderboard
2. Service team performance page
3. Customer rating capture
4. Bonus rules table
5. Daily waiter target tracking
6. Staff motivation dashboard
7. HRM performance reports
## Staff Motivation Logic
The system motivates the service team by tracking:
- sales amount
- number of orders served
- customer rating
- daily targets
- bonus eligibility
## Bonus Rule Examples
- Sales amount target
- Orders served target
- Customer rating target
## Business Value
This helps Meat Lovers:
- improve service quality
- reward high performers
- identify weak service staff
- increase sales through target-driven service
- create accountability for customer experience
## Enforcement Rule
Bonuses are not paid automatically.
This pack tracks eligibility and performance. Final bonus payment approval should remain under
management/finance control.
