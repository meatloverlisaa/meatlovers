## 📖 MAINTENANCE GUIDELINES

### For Future Development

When creating new pages or components:

1. **Always use approved colors:**
   - Primary: `bg-red-700`, `text-red-700`, `border-red-700`
   - Neutral: zinc, slate, stone
   - Success: `bg-emerald-700` (only for success states)
   - Warning: `bg-amber-600` (only for warnings)

2. **Never use:**
   - Blue colors
   - Purple colors
   - Cyan colors
   - Sky colors
   - Indigo colors
   - Violet colors
   - Fuchsia colors

3. **Sidebar navigation:**
   - Background: `bg-[#09090B]` or `bg-zinc-950`
   - Active state: `bg-red-700`
   - Inactive text: `text-zinc-400`

4. **Focus states:**
   - Use `focus:ring-red-700` for all inputs
   - Use `focus:border-red-700` for borders

5. **Gradients:**
   - Use `from-red-700 to-red-800` for red gradients
   - Avoid blue/cyan/purple gradients
